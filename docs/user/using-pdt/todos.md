# Todos

Your own todo list, beside the work: things to do yourself, with due dates and reminders. The PM can read it, to
plan with you, but only you change it.

## Open the list

The **Todos** panel sits on the right of the screen. **Ctrl+]** then **t** (or **⌘.** on a Mac) opens and closes it;
closed, it is a narrow strip with the number of open todos. Drag its edge to make it wider. On a phone, tap the
**Todos** button in the top bar.

The menu next to the heading picks what it shows: **All projects**, or only the project you're in.

## Add a todo

Type in **Add a todo** and press **Enter**. Dates, times and repeats in the text are picked up and highlighted:
"Call the bank fri 3pm", "Pay rent monthly", "Check the release in 2 weeks". They're taken out of the title; clear
the date's chip to keep the words in the title instead.

The chips under the box set the same things by hand: **Date** (with quick picks, a time and **Repeat**), **Repeat**,
**Project** and **Ticket** (link the todo to a ticket). A new todo goes in the project you're scoped to.

## Work through the list

Open todos are grouped by when they're due: **Overdue**, **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next 7 days**, **Later** and
**No date**. **Move to today** moves every overdue todo to today.

- **Tick the box** to finish a todo. It moves to **Completed** a moment later; **Clear** there deletes the finished
  ones. A repeating todo comes back with its next due date.
- **Click the title** to open the todo large, with its notes (Markdown, saved as you type) and the same controls.
  **Back** or **Escape** returns to the list.
- **Drag** a todo, or press **Alt+↑** / **Alt+↓**, to reorder it.
- Each row's **⋯** has **Edit**, **Due date…**, **Repeat…**, **Stop repeating**, **Link to a ticket…**, **Project** and
  **Delete**. Deleting a repeating todo asks whether to delete only this one or the whole series.

A todo linked to a ticket shows its key; click it to open the ticket.

## Repeating todos

**Repeat…** offers every day, every weekday, every week, every 2 weeks and every month, or **Custom…**: an interval,
the weekdays or the day of the month, and whether the next one counts from **The due date** or from **When it's
done**. A repeating todo needs a due date.

## Reminders

When a todo comes due (its day starts, or its time comes), PDT notifies you; opening the notification opens the todo.
To do something else when one comes due, use the **Todo due** trigger in a
[workflow](../automation/workflows.md#triggers).
