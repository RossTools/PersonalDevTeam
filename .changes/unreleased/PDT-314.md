A new **Documents** view keeps design docs, plans and spreadsheets outside your repository, linked to the projects and tickets they're about: browse, search and filter them, make or import one, and link it from either side. Each ticket has a **Documents** section with **Create design doc**, which makes a doc from a short template and links it, and agents working on the ticket are told to read it. Settings choose where new documents go. PDT's own local documents and Google Docs and Sheets come in later releases.
