The Inbox has a new look: every item shows its kind, ticket and links at the top, one line on why it waits, and its actions in a bar at the bottom. Agents' questions are cards you answer with a click or a number key (or **Something else…**), notices are coloured by severity, and the list has five sections: Questions, Approvals, Stuck, To act on and Ready to accept.
