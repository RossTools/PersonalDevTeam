Auto-start is now the built-in **Pick up tickets** workflow, with your project's auto-start settings as its settings: it starts the same tickets as before, and you can edit or copy it to pick up tickets your own way (only some labels, Ready tickets only at night, caps per label or area). A ticket whose start was cut short by a restart or a full disk is now handed on to its workflow instead of being left without one.
