Watch a ticket or a workflow run: ask the PM to keep an eye on one and it is told when the ticket's agent reports, the work finishes or fails, or it waits on you, then tells you, with no checking in between. You can watch one yourself with the eye at the top of a ticket or run, and hear about it in the Inbox's Updates and in desktop notifications; the page shows who watches it.
