Agents and the PM can now read and write your documents: they draft specs and plans as documents linked to the ticket, fill in sheets with values, formulas, formats and charts, and answer comments, asking you before each change unless the project lets the PM act. A document can open in tabs beside an agent's or the PM's conversation, opened by you or by the agent, so you watch its edits land; on a phone it is a pane beside the conversation.
